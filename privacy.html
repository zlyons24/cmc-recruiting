Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CMC Business Solutions LLC (d/b/a CMC Recruiting) ("CMC Recruiting," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit our website, contact us, or receive text messages from us.

Information We Collect

  • Information you provide: name, email address, mobile phone number, company or current role, résumé details, and any message you send us.
  • SMS consent records: whether you agreed to receive text messages, the date and time of consent, and the web page where consent was given.
  • Basic technical data: standard information your browser sends, such as browser type and pages visited.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide recruiting services.
  • To match candidates with sales opportunities and connect employers with candidates.
  • To send text messages you have agreed to receive, such as interview scheduling, application updates, and (if you opted in) job opportunity alerts.
  • To maintain records required by law and to improve our services.

SMS / Text Messaging Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing with subcontractors in support services, such as customer service and our SMS messaging provider, is permitted. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help. See our SMS Terms & Conditions for details.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only:

  • With employers or candidates as part of a recruiting process you have asked us to help with (for example, presenting a candidate's profile to a hiring company with the candidate's permission).
  • With service providers who help us operate our business (such as email, hosting, and SMS providers), who may use it only to provide services to us.
  • When required by law, or to protect our rights, safety, or property.

None of the above applies to SMS opt-in data and consent, which is never shared with third parties.

Data Security & Retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

Your Choices

  • Opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP.
  • Ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information by contacting us below.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last changed.
