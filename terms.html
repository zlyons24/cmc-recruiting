1. Program Name & Description

CMC Recruiting Text Messages. CMC Business Solutions LLC (d/b/a CMC Recruiting) sends text messages to candidates and hiring clients who opt in. Messages may include:

  • Recruiting / account messages: interview scheduling and reminders, application status updates, and replies to your inquiries.
  • Job alerts / promotional messages (only if you separately opt in): new sales job opportunities and updates about our recruiting services.

2. How You Opt In

You opt in by checking the SMS consent box(es) on the contact form on our website and submitting your mobile number. Consent checkboxes are optional and unchecked by default. Consent is not a condition of any purchase or service.

3. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your interactions with us (for example, active interview scheduling may involve several messages per week).

4. Costs

Message and data rates may apply. Check with your mobile carrier for details about your plan.

5. How to Opt Out

You can cancel at any time. Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. You will receive one final message confirming you have been unsubscribed. You may also reply CANCEL, END, QUIT, UNSUBSCRIBE, or STOPALL. To rejoin, opt in again through our website or reply START.

6. Help

Reply HELP to any message for assistance, or contact us at [email address] or [phone number].

7. Carrier Disclaimer

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

8. Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. SMS opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
